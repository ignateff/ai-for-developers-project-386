### Open generator experiments: results and stop gates

- [#43 — runtime glue](https://github.com/ignateff/ai-for-developers-project-386/issues/43) remains **open**. Its [published result](https://github.com/ignateff/ai-for-developers-project-386/issues/43#issuecomment-6561318077) reports all 10 operations registered and five retaining application validation; existing checks and built-server health passed, but container packaging and full equivalence were not checked. The branch and detailed report were local and unpublished. The experiment recommends not adopting it yet; it does not decide future adoption. Its acceptance gate disallows significant unapproved behavioral differences and requires documented validation limitations and comparison before any decision.
- [#44 — build-time generation](https://github.com/ignateff/ai-for-developers-project-386/issues/44) remains **open**. Its [published result](https://github.com/ignateff/ai-for-developers-project-386/issues/44#issuecomment-6561819866) stopped at the agreed compatibility gate: Walecloud 1.5.0 / openapi-typescript 7.13.0 required TypeScript 5 and type generation failed with the project's TypeScript 7. Route configurations were reproducible in two runs, but typed adapters, HTTP behavior and startup without glue/YAML were not verified. Continuing needs a separate decision on isolated TypeScript 5 or another generator; #44 also calls for stopping rather than silently bypassing contract loss, an API change or a full custom compiler.

Issue status and later evidence can change; check the linked issues before acting. Research recommendations, experiment results and accepted decisions above are deliberately distinct.
